# Logo capture critic

Use this skill only on a trusted capture when logo identity is the primary deliverable. Judge rendered evidence, not the Plan's claims.

Every requested criterion is non-compensating. Always judge black silhouette distinctiveness, anti-template integrity, counterform and contour quality, optical balance, real 16/24/32 px recognition, monochrome integrity, and brief fidelity. Judge concept divergence only for declared exploration, symbol-wordmark relationship only for requested Wordmark/lockups, app-icon optical adaptation only for requested App Icon output, and component/system integrity only for a multi-output system.

Judge calibration through the mark and requested system, not captions. Expressive cannot add arbitrary parts; restrained cannot excuse generic geometry; dense and airy boards must both preserve evaluable specimen hierarchy.

For declared exploration, score every direction independently. Each direction must expose an ownable silhouette, intentional construction logic, controlled contour or counterform, 32/24/16 px recognition, anti-template originality, and visible agreement with its thesis. Reject the entire exploration when any one direction is filler; stronger siblings and explanatory captions cannot compensate for it. Perform these checks in the same exact-revision critique rather than adding one model round trip per direction.

Reject three cosmetic variants of one letter, generic initials/circles/tiles/nodes/sparkles, captions carrying the idea, mathematically centered but optically unbalanced forms, large-only legibility, a default font merely placed beside the mark, scaled-down rather than redrawn icons, and repeated specimens that ignore declared Components.

When authorized visual references exist, require the declared transferable brand/style principles to be visible without reproducing another mark's silhouette, counterform, letter construction, or lockup. Superficial color borrowing is not reference adherence.

If any criterion fails, name the visible evidence and a concrete contour, spacing, hierarchy, or system edit. Attractive color, presentation polish, or explanatory prose cannot compensate for weak concept divergence or a generic mark.

# Logo visual direction

Use for a logo, symbol, wordmark, identity system, or derived app icon. It changes judgment, never authority.

- Compress the brief into brand difference, audience, territory, contexts, and exclusions.
- When references are attached, distinguish supplied brand constraints from style or composition inspiration. Extract only explicit transferable principles and avoid copying another mark, letter construction, silhouette, counterform, or lockup.
- Create three principles such as negative space, modular path, spatial relation, or symbol-wordmark type. Color, radius, weight, aperture, rotation, and arrangement are cosmetic. Each needs a thesis, construction, editable root, monochrome proof, and 16/24/32 px tests.
- Reject generic letter containers, alphabet variants, circles, nodes, sparkles, gradients, and caption-dependent marks. A letter passes only with an ownable silhouette or counterform.
- Select by distinctiveness, fit, reproducibility, scalability, and system potential. Refine contour, optical balance, clear space, monochrome use, and wordmark spacing.
- Redraw the app icon optically. Use Component masters and linked specimen Instances.
